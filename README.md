# Guns of Marz Extension: Guns of Tien

Project Zomboid Build 42 mod.

**Requires:** Gunworks Gang Framework, Guns of Marz, Ammo Maker
**Optional:** Hot Brass (spent casings)

## New ammunition

Each in boxes, cartons and crates, with Ammo Maker recipes. Like Guns of Marz's own special loads they are not
found as loot: make them with Ammo Maker.

- 7.62x39mm US (Subsonic), Soft Point, Steel Core (Armor Piercing)
- 9x19mm 147gr (Subsonic), Hollow Point, [+P+]
- .45 ACP Hollow Point, [+P]
- 9x39mm SP-6 (Armor Piercing)
- .30-06 Springfield Soft Point
- .308 Winchester Soft Point
- 12 Gauge Birdshot

## Changes to Guns of Marz

- Realistic sound radius and volume for the guns (not the grenade launchers)
- Plain .30-06, 7.62x51mm and .308 rounds pierce (sandbox option)
- 7.62x39mm US (Subsonic) must be racked after each shot unless a PBS-1 is fitted (sandbox option)
- 9x39mm round renamed SP-5, 5.45x39mm round renamed 7N6
- Lasers work: switch them on from the gun's menu or the firearm radial; while on, the laser shows a short red
  beam on the gun, also to other players; they run on a battery (about 3 weeks switched on, changed like a weapon
  light's) and give their aiming bonus only while on
- Clear tooltips: stock and bipod lines state what deploying them does; part rows that showed raw keys are named;
  every round's lines are written from its real stats (Guns of Marz's had wrong wear numbers and left critical changes
  out); bayonets, the shell holder, the Picatinny Rail, speedloaders, slings and guns that had no lines say what they do
- Guns of Marz fixes: the Karabiner 98k bayonet fits the guns the other bayonets fit, and the overpressured 5.56's
  automatic fire spread penalty applies
- Sawn-off stocks: the break-action shotguns (Double Barrel, Stevens 555, TOZ-34) can have their wooden stock sawn
  off behind the pistol grip with any saw (Saw Off Shotgun Stock); lighter and quicker to aim in, but less accurate
  and slower between shots; with sawn-off barrels too they fit a holster. The sawn-off stock and barrel ends are kept
  as offcuts: wood to burn or make charcoal, steel to smelt. The Stevens 555's and TOZ-34's sawn-off barrels handle
  like the Double Barrel's (GoM gave only those any stats)

## Ammo Maker

- Recipes for every new round
- Soft point bullets: Make Soft Point Bullet
- Birdshot pellets: Pour Birdshot Pellets

## Optional add-on: Post-93

Enable *GoM Extension: Guns of Tien - Post-93* as well. Its guns, magazines, parts and standard ammunition are found
as loot the way Guns of Marz's are (scaled by Guns of Marz's own loot multiplier); its special loads only from Ammo Maker.

- H&K MP7A1 and MP7A2: 20, 30 and 40-round magazines, 60-round drum, collapsible stock, suppressor; the A1 with its folding front grip, the A2 with a rail handguard that takes Guns of Marz's foregrips
- FN P90: 50-round magazine, suppressor
- Both held like pistols, carried on the back, take Guns of Marz optics, lights and lasers
- FN Five-seveN pistol (Guns of Marz's own name for it): 20 and 30-round magazines, its own SFN-57 suppressor,
  takes Guns of Marz's pistol sights and pistol lights
- H&K UCP Prototype: the MP7's 4.6x30mm companion pistol that H&K never sold, 20-round magazine and a small 40-round drum, takes the MP7's
  suppressor and Guns of Marz's pistol sights and pistol lights
- 4.6x30mm DM11, Subsonic
- 5.7x28mm SS190, SB193 (Subsonic), SS197SR (Hollow Point)
