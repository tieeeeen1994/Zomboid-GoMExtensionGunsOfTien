# Guns of Marz Extension: Guns of Tien

Project Zomboid Build 42 mod.

**Requires:** Gunworks Gang Framework, Guns of Marz
**Optional:** Ammo Maker (making the new rounds), Hot Brass (spent casings)

Every gun this mod and Post-93 add is drawn true to the character's size, in the proportion of Guns of Marz's M16A2.

## New ammunition

Each in boxes, cartons and crates. Like Guns of Marz's own special loads they are not found as loot. With Ammo
Maker they can be made.

- 7.62x39mm US (Subsonic), Soft Point, Steel Core (Armor Piercing)
- 9x19mm 147gr (Subsonic), Hollow Point, [+P+]
- .45 ACP Hollow Point, [+P]
- 9x39mm SP-6 (Armor Piercing)
- .30-06 Springfield Soft Point
- .308 Winchester Soft Point
- 12 Gauge Birdshot

## Changes to Guns of Marz

- Realistic sound radius and volume for the guns (not the grenade launchers)
- Plain .30-06, 7.62x51mm and .308 rounds pierce (sandbox option)
- 7.62x39mm US (Subsonic) must be racked after each shot unless a PBS-1 is fitted (sandbox option)
- 9x39mm round renamed SP-5, 5.45x39mm round renamed 7N6
- Guns of Marz's guns can be drawn to scale: in proportion to the character and to each other, on the M16A2's scale
  (Guns of Marz draws most of its guns larger); guns that take the same magazines are drawn the same size. Not the
  MP5K or the Stolzer & Son 2-Bore (sandbox option, off by default) (beta)
- Lasers work: switch them on from the gun's menu or the firearm radial; while on, the laser shows a short red
  beam on the gun, also to other players; they run on a battery (about 3 weeks switched on, changed like a weapon
  light's) and give their aiming bonus only while on
- Clear tooltips: stock and bipod lines state what deploying them does; part rows that showed raw keys are named;
  every round's lines are written from its real stats (Guns of Marz's had wrong wear numbers and left critical changes
  out); bayonets, the shell holder, the Picatinny Rail, speedloaders, slings and guns that had no lines say what they do
- AWC Mk9 suppressor for 9mm submachine guns, on its own muzzle mount: fits the MP5, MP5A2 and this mod's Uzis
  (Guns of Marz's 9mm suppressors fit only its pistols), found where Guns of Marz's suppressors and muzzle devices are
- Guns of Marz fixes: the Karabiner 98k bayonet fits the guns the other bayonets fit, and the overpressured 5.56's
  automatic fire spread penalty applies
- Sawn-off stocks: the break-action shotguns (Double Barrel, Stevens 555, TOZ-34) can have their wooden stock sawn
  off behind the pistol grip with any saw (Saw Off Shotgun Stock); lighter and quicker to aim in, but less accurate
  and slower between shots; with sawn-off barrels too they fit a holster. The sawn-off stock and barrel ends are kept
  as offcuts: wood to burn or make charcoal, steel to smelt. The Stevens 555's and TOZ-34's sawn-off barrels handle
  like the Double Barrel's (GoM gave only those any stats)

## M249 Light Machine Gun and belt links

- M249 Light Machine Gun (the 1993 PIP gun): full auto at 750 or 1000 rounds a minute (its gas regulator), fed from
  its own 200-round box or, in an emergency, any of Guns of Marz's STANAG magazines (which jam twice as often),
  integrated bipod; found where Guns of Marz's M60 is (army zombies, army storage, heavy army trucks), with its boxes
- Belt links: filling the M249's 200-round box or Guns of Marz's M60 100-round box now takes one belt link per round
  (M27 links for the M249, M13 for the M60). Unloading a round, firing it or racking it out gives its link back: fired
  and racked links are thrown out of the gun (with Hot Brass they fly and land like casings, without it they drop at
  your feet; Ammo Maker's brass catchers catch them). A belt box found loaded counts as linked
- Links come in boxes (200 M27, 100 M13) in the M249 and M60 kits and in army ammunition storage, or are made at any
  surface from a small sheet of metal with sheet metal snips, a hammer and a metalworking punch (Make M27 5.56mm or
  M13 7.62mm Belt Links, Metalworking 2: 50 M27 or 40 M13 links)

## Uzi, Mini Uzi and Micro Uzi

- Uzi (its metal stock folding under the receiver) and Mini Uzi (fitting a holster), both held two-handed in the short
  SMG stance below, and Micro Uzi (held one-handed and fitting a holster like the MAC-10, also an ankle holster);
  the Mini's and Micro's stocks fold along the right side: automatic at 600, 950 and 1200 rounds a minute, or single
  shots
- One set of IMI magazines fits all three: 20, 25 and 32 rounds, and long 40 and 50-round magazines
- The AWC Mk9 suppressor fits all three on its 9mm SMG muzzle mount
- Found with Guns of Marz's short SMGs (gun stores, pawn shops, survivalists, bandits); the Uzi also with its police
  SMGs and on SWAT zombies, and with its criminals' guns (criminal zombies, bandit bags, pawn shops, stashes); the long
  magazines with its high-capacity magazines

## Short SMG stance (beta)

- The Uzi, Mini Uzi, Guns of Marz's MP5K and Post-93's MP7s share one stance of their own: carried with both hands
  down across the body, magazine hanging clear of the belly; aimed like a rifle with the support hand closer in on the
  shorter gun (on the foregrip of the MP5K and of the MP7A1 when its grip is unfolded)
- Every animation of the rifle stance is remade for it: idle, sneaking, walking, running, sprinting, aiming at every
  angle and while moving, shooting, shoving, vaulting, falling, getting up, sitting, idle fidgets and emotes
- Their own reloads: the magazine is drawn out and carried in the hand, and each gun is racked by its own handle
- The Mini Uzi, the MP5K and Post-93's MP7s go into both hands by default; "Equip Primary" still holds them in one
  hand, like a pistol, with the pistol reload (the Mini Uzi still fits a holster); the Micro Uzi stays a one-handed
  machine pistol

## Ammo Maker (optional)

Used when it is enabled, as in Guns of Marz. Without it the new rounds cannot be made.

- Recipes for every new round
- Soft point bullets: Make Soft Point Bullet
- Birdshot pellets: Pour Birdshot Pellets

Removing Ammo Maker from an existing save deletes Ammo Maker's own items (gunpowder, casings, bullets, tools) as
players log in and areas load, as for any removed mod. Guns of Tien's rounds stay.

## Optional add-on: Post-93

Enable *GoM Extension: Guns of Tien - Post-93* as well. Its guns, magazines, parts and standard ammunition are found
as loot the way Guns of Marz's are (scaled by Guns of Marz's own loot multiplier); its special loads only from Ammo Maker.

- H&K MP7A1 and MP7A2: 20, 30 and 40-round magazines, 60-round drum, collapsible stock, suppressor; the A1 with its folding front grip, the A2 with a rail handguard that takes Guns of Marz's foregrips
- FN P90: 50-round magazine, suppressor
- The MP7s held in the main mod's short SMG stance, the P90 like a pistol; both carried on the back, take Guns of
  Marz optics, lights and lasers
- FN Five-seveN pistol (Guns of Marz's own name for it): 20 and 30-round magazines, its own SFN-57 suppressor,
  takes Guns of Marz's pistol sights and pistol lights
- H&K UCP Prototype: the MP7's 4.6x30mm companion pistol that H&K never sold, 20-round magazine and a small 40-round drum, takes the MP7's
  suppressor and Guns of Marz's pistol sights and pistol lights
- FN SCAR-L (Mk 16) and SCAR-H (Mk 17, long barrel) in flat dark earth: side-folding stock, rails all round for Guns of Marz optics, foregrips, bipods, lights and lasers; the SCAR-L takes Guns of Marz's STANAG magazines and AR muzzle parts, the SCAR-H its own 20 and 10-round magazines, a 50-round drum and an SOCOM762-RC suppressor
- H&K UMP45 and UMP9: one polymer body with each gun's own magazines (UMP45 25, 20 and 10-round translucent .45 ACP magazines; UMP9 30-round 9mm), side-folding stock, two-round burst, rails all round for Guns of Marz optics, foregrips, lights and lasers; the UMP45 takes Guns of Marz's .45 muzzle mount and P45 suppressor, the UMP9 the AWC Mk9
- 4.6x30mm DM11, Subsonic
- 5.7x28mm SS190, SB193 (Subsonic), SS197SR (Hollow Point)
