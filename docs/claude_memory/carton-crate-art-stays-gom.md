Guns of Tien's ammo cartons and crates keep Guns of Marz's shared art (vanilla Parcel_Medium_Ammo / Parcel_Medium_Military, GoM's Ammo_Crate), even though loads look alike.

**Why:** On 2026-11-13 two versions were built and both reverted. Colour stickers per load were "even more confusing due to special ammo types", because one colour meant the same load across calibres. Printed calibre plus a sticker, on GoM's cartons too, "looks ugly".

**How to apply:** Don't propose per-load carton/crate art again unless the user brings it up. If they do, show a mock-up before wiring anything in. Related: [[follow-gom-patterns]].
