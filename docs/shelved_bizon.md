# Shelved: PP-19 Bizon family (Post-93)

Shelved 2026-11-26 by the user ("let's drop the bizon todo. i'll do them later"). Nothing built. It was item 2 of the
"Next guns" list locked on 2026-11-23, after the Tokarev and Makarov releases.

## The plan as it stood

- One gun per real version, named by its round: **Bizon Parabellum** (Bizon-2-01, 9x19, 53-round helical magazine),
  **Bizon Makarov** (Bizon-2, 9x18, 64-round), **Bizon Tokarev** (Bizon-2-07, 7.62x25), and if their rounds come,
  the .380 Bizon-2-02 and the suppressed 9x18 Bizon-2-03.
- Each its own helical magazine: the calibres differ in barrel and bolt too, so no magazine swap between them.
- Built on GoM's AKS-74U (the real gun is mostly AKS-74 parts), held as a rifle, GoM's AK reload.
- Rounds: 9x18mm Makarov exists since 2026-11-25 (main mod, plain only; boxes TUSKER), first used by the Skorpion
  vz. 82 and the PP-91 Kedr. Its later loads (PMM 1994, SP-7, RG028) are Post-93. 7.62x25 exists with the TT-33 /
  PPSh-41. The same 9x18 would also serve the CZ-83 and the PP-9 Klin (a strengthened Kedr for the PMM load: the
  Kedr's model with other markings).

## Research (2026-11-26, web; not checked against a Russian spec sheet)

No Bizon has a rifle-length barrel; all are SMG length.

| Version | Round | Barrel |
|---|---|---|
| Bizon (1993) | 9x18 | 195 mm |
| Bizon-2 | 9x18 Makarov | 230 mm |
| Bizon-2-01 | 9x19 | 225 mm |
| Bizon-2-03 | 9x18, integral suppressor (expansion chamber over the barrel, longer front) | not found |
| Bizon-2-04 | 9x18, semi-auto carbine | not found |
| Bizon-2-06 (some list -02) | .380 ACP, semi-auto carbine | not found |
| Bizon-2-07 | 7.62x25 Tokarev, developed 1997 | not found |

The Tokarev Bizon was real. Sources disagree on the -02 / -06 / -07 numbering; one Russian page calls the -07 a
semi-auto line in 9x17, 9x18 and 9x19. Whether the civilian carbines (-04, -06) had longer barrels was not found.

imfdb.org/wiki/PP-19_Bizon-2, en.valka.cz/topic/view/8991.
