[h1]Guns of Marz Extension: Guns of Tien: everything it adds and changes[/h1]

More ammunition, guns and fixes for Guns of Marz, set in Kentucky, July 1993: only loads and guns that existed then (the optional Post-93 add-on has the later ones).

[b]Requires:[/b] Gunworks Gang Framework, Guns of Marz
[b]Optional:[/b] Ammo Maker (making the new rounds), Hot Brass (spent casings)

Every gun this mod and Post-93 add is drawn true to the character's size, in the proportion of Guns of Marz's M16A2, and is held and reloaded with Guns of Marz's own animations (the closest Guns of Marz gun's); as on the real guns, the charging handle stays forward while firing and moves only when the gun is racked, while the bolt moves in the ejection port on each shot and stays back on an empty gun (the SCARs' handle rides with their bolt carrier, as on the real gun).

Found a problem? Post it in this thread and say which gun or item, whether it was single player or a server, and what you did.

[h2].22 LR[/h2]
A new calibre, .22 Long Rifle, the small rimfire round of plinkers and small game.
[list]
[*]The plain round (40 grain lead round nose), in boxes of 50, cartons and crates
[*]Special loads, not found as loot and made with Ammo Maker: Subsonic (quieter and a little weaker; must be racked after each shot in self-loading guns, not in revolvers or bolt, lever and pump actions), Hollow Point, and Hyper Velocity (hits harder, crits harder, louder, and wears the gun a little faster)
[*]Every .22 LR gun fires a light, sharp report, well under a 9mm's
[*]With Hot Brass its spent casings fly out like any other
[*]Fired by the Walther PPK (below) and, with the Post-93 add-on, the HK416 .22 Quad Rail Pistol
[*]Plain .22 LR boxes and cartons turn up wherever 9mm boxes and cartons do, homes included
[/list]

[h2]Changes to Guns of Marz[/h2]
[list]
[*]Realistic sound radius and volume for the guns (not the grenade launchers)
[*]Plain .30-06, 7.62x51mm and .308 rounds pierce (sandbox option)
[*]7.62x39mm US (Subsonic) must be racked after each shot unless a PBS-1 is fitted (sandbox option)
[*]Gun critical hits when one shot hits several targets (shotgun pellets, rounds that pierce): Build 42.22 rolls once per shot, so a blast crits on every pellet or on none; Old rolls for each target again, as before Build 42.22, and Enhanced rolls for each target but led by the trigger pull's roll (sandbox option, Default = Build 42.22's way)
[*]Minimum firearm damage: since Build 42.22 a gun's failed hit roll on a zombie only staggers it for no damage; this gives such a hit a share of the damage it would have done (after body part, clothing and range, never a critical hit). 100% is Build 42.21's every-hit-lands, which came from a bug (sandbox option, 0% = the game's rule by default)
[*]Lasers work: switch them on from the gun's menu or the firearm radial; while on, the laser shows a short red beam on the gun, also to other players; they run on a battery (about 3 weeks switched on, changed like a weapon light's) and give their aiming bonus only while on
[*]Bayonet usage (off by default): a gun with a bayonet fitted (or the SKS's folding one) becomes a spear with Use Bayonet: its own key (Use / Stop Using Bayonet in the controls, Z by default), the firearm radial or the gun's menu; Stop Using Bayonet (key, radial or menu) gives the gun back with its ammunition, magazine, parts and hotbar slot. While the bayonet is in use the gun cannot fire; it stabs with Gunworks' bayonet stats, trains Spear, Strength and Fitness (also on a server, where bayonet hits gave no XP before) and wears the bayonet, which comes off broken at 0. A shove or a sprint no longer stabs (sandbox option Toggle Bayonet Usage)
[*]Clear tooltips: stock and bipod lines state what deploying them does; part rows that showed raw keys are named; every round's lines are written from its real stats; bayonets, the shell holder, the Picatinny Rail, speedloaders, slings and guns that had no lines say what they do; every gun's lines, Guns of Marz's included, follow one format written from what the gun really takes (calibre, built-in parts, integrated rails, rail slots, optic limits, mounting and muzzle devices, bayonet), so none of them can promise a rail the gun does not have; every magazine lists the guns it fits
[*]AWC Mk9 suppressor, with the stats of Guns of Marz's 9mm suppressors, found where Guns of Marz's suppressors are. Every 9mm suppressor fits every 9mm pistol and submachine gun that takes the mount. All 9mm guns share one muzzle mount, Guns of Marz's pistol mount renamed "9x19mm Family Muzzle Mounting Device"; mounts of the old 9mm SMG kind in a world become that one by themselves
[*]Every gun takes its calibre's muzzle mount wherever something of that family fits it: Guns of Marz's flash hider, compensator and muzzle brake on every 5.56mm gun with the AR mount (the FN FNC, Mini-14, Model 70, XM177, AUG A1 and M249 too), and suppressors only where the real gun takes one: the 5.56mm suppressors on the Model 70, XM177 and AUG A1 (not the M249: real ones are machine-gun suppressors), the PBS-1 on the SKS, the P45 on the Thompson and Camp Carbine, every 9mm suppressor on the TEC-9, and with the Post-93 add-on the SOCOM762-RC on the SR-25, FN FAL, G3, M14, M24, PSG-1 and Remington 700. No mount on the RPD, M60, AUG HBAR, Colt Single Action Army, MP5K or MP5SD, where nothing would fit
[*]Real muzzle devices, each fitting only the guns the real one was made for, on its calibre's muzzle mount: the AKM slant compensator (AK-47, RPK, SKS), the AK-74 muzzle brake and AKS-74U muzzle booster (AK-74, AKS-74U, RPK-74), H&K's three-lug flash hider (MP5A3, MP5A2), Clark race compensators for 9mm pistols (Beretta 92FS, Hi-Power, P226) and the M1911, and the Thompson's Cutts compensator; with the Post-93 add-on SureFire's SF3P flash hider (FN FAL, FN SCAR-H, G3, HK417, M14, M24, MDRx .308, Mk 11, PSG-1, Remington 700, SR-25), SFMB muzzle brake and WarComp (FN SCAR-H, G3, HK417, M24, MDRx .308, Mk 11, PSG-1, Remington 700, SR-25); the Grozas take the AK ones. Each removes the muzzle flash and lowers the sound a little (compensators Sound Radius by 15 and Volume by 4, hiders and brakes by 10 and 2, as Guns of Marz's own), and does its real job: compensators Aiming Time -3 and automatic fire spread -10%, muzzle brakes Aiming Time -5 and spread -5%, flash hiders Hit Chance +2%, Guns of Marz's LR2, LX and Trix42 included. Found where Guns of Marz's muzzle devices are
[*]Guns of Marz's MP5 is named for what it is, the MP5A3 (retractable stock)
[*]Follows Guns of Marz's November 2026 update on every gun of this mod and the Post-93 add-on: each gun has its class's optimal range (submachine guns 3-11 tiles, machine pistols 2-10, carbines 4-13 or 5-14, assault rifles 7-17, battle rifles and machine guns 9-20, marksman rifles 11-23, the SV-98 as the M24 14-28, pistols 2-8); stocks, foregrips, scopes and the AUG's sight give Guns of Marz's flat bonuses (and Aiming Time never drops below 5), the built-in bipods change no stats as Guns of Marz's do; every semi-automatic and bolt gun fires one shot per click; suppressors wear (a bar on the gun's tooltip shows how far), break and are repaired with a Repair Pack or a second can, broken ones too, and a found suppressor or muzzle device comes with its mount; scopes, lasers, lights, foregrips, bipods, suppressors and muzzle devices need no tool once their mount is on; the two-handed submachine guns and bullpups (UMPs, Vector .45 and Carbine, AUGs, MDRx, PS90, SVU, Groza-1 and Groza-4) are held with Guns of Marz's MP5 / FAMAS stance and its foregrip stance; the AUG's and MP7A1's front grips fold with Gunworks' Swap; the M249, SCAR-L, MDRx .223 and HK416 take both sides of Guns of Marz's jungle-taped STANAG pair and draw its new long magazines; the HK416, HK417s, SR-25 and Mk 11 hold their bolt open after the last round, and the reload that follows ends with a slap on the bolt catch, as on Guns of Marz's M16s and M4A1
[*]Guns of Marz fixes: the Karabiner 98k bayonet fits the guns the other bayonets fit, and the overpressured 5.56's automatic fire spread penalty applies; on 22 guns the muzzle flash and the bullet start at the end of the barrel (they started a little ahead of it or inside it); opening and packing ammo boxes, cartons and crates can be done while walking again (since 42.22 the game ignores the old walking setting Guns of Marz's recipes use); on a server, racking after a reload no longer leaves the gun saying "Chamber is open" with a round chambered (a Gunworks timing bug that cut racks short for skilled characters, seen most on the UMPs, Vectors and SCARs), and a rack cut short (a trigger pull or a sprint while the bolt is back) closes the bolt instead of leaving the gun stuck on that message; a gun still left that way with no bolt to close is freed by pulling the trigger, and on a server the M16s, M4A1, CAR-15, XM177, AR-15, SKS and M1 Garand show their bolt held open after the last round (it was drawn closed while the gun counted as open); the AA-12's side rails, lights and lasers and the G36's bipod are drawn (they were fitted invisibly), the M16A2 takes the AimRight laser its loot already gave it, and the PSG-1 and SVD the Stub, MKC and MK2 foregrips theirs gave them; the bipod no longer fits the G36C, M4A1, XM177 or the MP5s (it overlapped the foregrip or had no room), and a bipod already fitted on one comes off and lies beside the gun (sandbox option Update Parts on Saved Guns, on by default, which also swaps the old 9mm SMG mount; off once a world has been played through saves the checks)
[*]Sawn-off stocks: the break-action shotguns (Double Barrel, Stevens 555, TOZ-34) can have their wooden stock sawn off behind the pistol grip with any saw (Saw Off Shotgun Stock); lighter and quicker to aim in, but less accurate and slower between shots; with sawn-off barrels too they fit a holster. The sawn-off stock and barrel ends are kept as offcuts: wood to burn or make charcoal, steel to smelt. The Stevens 555's and TOZ-34's sawn-off barrels handle like the Double Barrel's
[*]Mosin-Nagant Obrez: Guns of Marz's Mosin-Nagant can have its stock sawn off (Saw Off Rifle Stock, any saw) and its barrel and forend sawn off ahead of the rear sight (Saw Off Rifle Barrel, a metal saw), each on its own. The short barrel hits less hard, reaches half as far, is louder with a bigger flash and loses the bayonet lug, but aims in faster; with both cuts it fits a holster. The stock, barrel and forend are kept as offcuts
[*]A sawn-off gun is named and drawn for what was cut ("Mosin-Nagant Obrez", "Sawn-Off Double Barrel Pistol"), with its own icon
[*]Guns held like a pistol (Post-93's MP7s and P90) hang the right way up on attachment slots other mods add, such as Plysken Attachments Reborn's backpack slots, and sawn-off shotguns in modded holsters (sandbox option)
[*]9x39mm round renamed SP-5, 5.45x39mm round renamed 7N6
[*]Extend Loot Areas (off by default): zombies carry guns far more often and every gun, Guns of Marz's included, turns up in more places that bend reality but still make sense in game: most police officers, soldiers, bandits, hunters, park rangers, survivalists and militia armed (vanilla arms about one officer in four and one soldier or hunter in fifty), ghillie snipers always with their rifle; Guns of Marz's post-1993 guns (G36, Benelli M4, AA-12, Rhino, MP-412) in gun stores and SWAT armouries and on police and civilian zombies, its short submachine guns in shoulder holsters and drug labs, its imported pistols in holsters, its Russian rifles with criminals and survivalists, its M79 and M60 with more soldiers, its Double Deuce with hunters and its cowboy rifles in gun stores and ranger stations; the guns that were still rare (crowded spawners such as the police submachine guns, guns that shared one slot with their siblings such as the M4A1) are found far more often, in containers and on zombies (sandbox option)
[*]Gun Loot (%) (100 by default): how often guns turn up, from every mod (Guns of Marz's, Guns of Tien's, the game's own and any other mod's), in containers and on zombies, from 0 (no guns at all) to 200 (about twice as many); magazines, ammunition and parts are not changed (sandbox option)
[/list]

[h2]M249 Light Machine Gun and belt links[/h2]
[list]
[*]M249 Light Machine Gun (the 1993 PIP gun): full auto at 750 or 1000 rounds a minute (its gas regulator), fed from its own 200-round box or, in an emergency, any of Guns of Marz's STANAG magazines (which jam twice as often), integrated bipod; found where Guns of Marz's M60 is (army zombies, army storage, heavy army trucks), with its boxes
[*]Guns of Marz's Picatinny rails fit all four sides, on the feed cover and round the handguard: its optics (all but the four longest scopes, which would reach the carrying handle), foregrips, lights and lasers
[*]Guns of Marz's AR muzzle mount with its flash hider, compensator and muzzle brake (no suppressor)
[*]Belt links: filling the M249's 200-round box or Guns of Marz's M60 100-round box now takes one belt link per round (M27 links for the M249, M13 for the M60). Unloading a round gives its link back; a fired round's link comes out with its casing and goes wherever the casing goes: with Hot Brass it flies out of the gun and lands like the casing (gone after landing if Hot Brass's Persistent Casings is off, in the seat if you shoot from a vehicle), with Ammo Maker it ends up with its casing (your brass catcher, the floor, or Hot Brass's throw) and is cleaned up after Ammo Maker's Dropped Casings Lifetime like the casings, and with neither (no casings in vanilla) it is gone. A round racked out takes its link with it, into your inventory or out of the gun with Hot Brass. A belt box found loaded counts as linked
[*]Links come in boxes (200 M27, 100 M13) in the M249 and M60 kits and in army ammunition storage, or are made at any surface from a small sheet of metal with sheet metal snips, a hammer and a metalworking punch (Make M27 5.56mm or M13 7.62mm Belt Links, Metalworking 2: 50 M27 or 40 M13 links)
[*]Links can be switched off (sandbox option): the belt boxes then load like any magazine, and no link boxes are found
[/list]

[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
On the [b]Guns of Tien[/b] page of the sandbox settings.
[list]
[*][i]Adjusted Piercing[/i] (on by default): plain (full metal jacket) .30-06, 7.62x51mm and .308 rounds pierce in every gun that fires them, and plain .30-30 rounds (flat-nosed soft points in reality) no longer do. Off = Guns of Marz's own piercing
[*][i]Upright Guns in Modded Attachment Slots[/i] (on by default): guns held like a pistol hang upright on other mods' rifle slots, sawn-off shotguns in their holster slots. Takes effect the next time the game is loaded
[*][i]7.62x39mm US Subsonic Cycling[/i]: always rack after every shot, rack unless a PBS-1 is fitted (default), or never rack
[*][i]Belt-Fed Guns Need Links[/i] (on by default): the M249's and M60's belt boxes take one link per round. Off = they load like any magazine, no link boxes are found and the link recipes are hidden. Takes effect the next time the game is loaded
[*][i]Ammo Maker Part Cartons and Crates[/i] (on by default): with Ammo Maker, 15 boxes or bags of one kind of ammunition part pack into a carton and 5 cartons into a crate. Off = no packing, cartons and crates already packed can still be opened. Takes effect the next time the game is loaded
[*][i]Gun Critical Hits[/i]: when one shot hits several targets, Default (the game's rule since Build 42.22: one roll per shot decides every target), Old (as before Build 42.22: every target rolls at its own chance) or Enhanced (every target rolls, led by the trigger pull: a critical pull raises the other targets' chance, a plain one lowers it, and on average each target still crits at its own chance). A shot that hits one target is the same under every choice
[*][i]Trigger Pull Lead for Gun Critical Hits[/i] (50 by default): how strongly the trigger pull leads under Enhanced, from 0 (each target rolls on its own) to 100 (every target copies the trigger pull). At 50, a target with a 30% chance crits at 65% after a critical pull and at 15% after a plain one
[*][i]Minimum Firearm Damage (%)[/i] (0 by default): how much damage a gun's failed hit roll on a zombie still does, as a share of the same hit landed. 0 = the game's rule since Build 42.22 (a stagger for no damage). 100 = every hit that reaches a zombie does full damage, as in Build 42.21, where it was a bug. Players and animals are not changed, nothing changes while Firearms Use Damage Chance is Disabled, and such hits still give Aiming experience
[*][i]Toggle Bayonet Usage[/i] (off by default): Use / Stop Using Bayonet turns a gun with a bayonet into a spear and back. Off = Guns of Marz's own bayonet (a shove or a sprint stabs, with no experience on a server). Takes effect at once, and a gun whose bayonet is in use can still be given back
[*][i]Extend Loot Areas[/i] (off by default): zombies carry guns far more often and every gun, Guns of Marz's and Post-93's included, turns up in more places that bend reality but still make sense in game (see Changes to Guns of Marz and Post-93 above). Guns of Marz's own guns get rarer in the spawners other guns join. Takes effect the next time the game is loaded
[*][i]Update Parts on Saved Guns[/i] (on by default): guns saved with a part they no longer take (Guns of Marz's bipod on the G36C, M4A1, XM177 and MP5s) have it taken off and put beside them, and the old 9mm SMG muzzle mount is swapped for the 9x19mm Family Muzzle Mounting Device, on guns and loose; each area is checked as it loads, each player's inventory 10 seconds after joining and each vehicle once loaded. Off = nothing is checked, which saves that work once a world has been played through (an old mount still works as the new one). Takes effect at once
[*][i]Gun Loot (%)[/i] (100 by default): how often guns turn up, from every mod, in containers and on zombies. 0 = no guns spawn at all, 200 = about twice as many in containers (zombies as many more as their outfits allow). Magazines, ammunition and parts are not changed; Guns of Marz's own Loot Multiplier still applies to its guns. Takes effect the next time the game is loaded
[/list]
