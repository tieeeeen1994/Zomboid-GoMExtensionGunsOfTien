[h1]Guns of Marz Extension: Guns of Tien: everything it adds and changes[/h1]

More ammunition, guns and fixes for Guns of Marz, set in Kentucky, July 1993: only loads and guns that existed then (the optional Post-93 add-on has the later ones).

[b]Requires:[/b] Gunworks Gang Framework, Guns of Marz
[b]Optional:[/b] Ammo Maker (making the new rounds), Hot Brass (spent casings)

Every gun this mod and Post-93 add is drawn true to the character's size, in the proportion of Guns of Marz's M16A2, and is held and reloaded with Guns of Marz's own animations (the closest Guns of Marz gun's); as on the real guns, the charging handle stays forward while firing and moves only when the gun is racked, while the bolt moves in the ejection port on each shot and stays back on an empty gun (the SCARs' handle rides with their bolt carrier, as on the real gun).

Found a problem? Post it in this thread and say which gun or item, whether it was single player or a server, and what you did.

[h2]Changes to Guns of Marz[/h2]
[list]
[*]Realistic sound radius and volume for the guns (not the grenade launchers)
[*]Plain .30-06, 7.62x51mm and .308 rounds pierce (sandbox option)
[*]7.62x39mm US (Subsonic) must be racked after each shot unless a PBS-1 is fitted (sandbox option)
[*]Lasers work: switch them on from the gun's menu or the firearm radial; while on, the laser shows a short red beam on the gun, also to other players; they run on a battery (about 3 weeks switched on, changed like a weapon light's) and give their aiming bonus only while on
[*]Clear tooltips: stock and bipod lines state what deploying them does; part rows that showed raw keys are named; every round's lines are written from its real stats; bayonets, the shell holder, the Picatinny Rail, speedloaders, slings and guns that had no lines say what they do
[*]AWC Mk9 suppressor for 9mm submachine guns, on its own muzzle mount: fits the MP5, MP5A2 and this mod's Uzis (Guns of Marz's 9mm suppressors fit only its pistols), found where Guns of Marz's suppressors and muzzle devices are
[*]The MP5 family (MP5A3, MP5A2, MP5SD, MP5K) and its magazines drawn true to the character's size, in the Uzis' proportion, so the Mk9 sits on them as it does on the Uzis; Guns of Marz's MP5 is named for what it is, the MP5A3 (retractable stock)
[*]Guns of Marz fixes: the Karabiner 98k bayonet fits the guns the other bayonets fit, and the overpressured 5.56's automatic fire spread penalty applies; on 21 guns the muzzle flash and the bullet start at the end of the barrel (they started a little ahead of it or inside it)
[*]Sawn-off stocks: the break-action shotguns (Double Barrel, Stevens 555, TOZ-34) can have their wooden stock sawn off behind the pistol grip with any saw (Saw Off Shotgun Stock); lighter and quicker to aim in, but less accurate and slower between shots; with sawn-off barrels too they fit a holster. The sawn-off stock and barrel ends are kept as offcuts: wood to burn or make charcoal, steel to smelt. The Stevens 555's and TOZ-34's sawn-off barrels handle like the Double Barrel's
[*]Mosin-Nagant Obrez: Guns of Marz's Mosin-Nagant can have its stock sawn off (Saw Off Rifle Stock, any saw) and its barrel and forend sawn off ahead of the rear sight (Saw Off Rifle Barrel, a metal saw), each on its own. The short barrel hits less hard, reaches half as far, is louder with a bigger flash and loses the bayonet lug, but aims in faster; with both cuts it fits a holster. The stock, barrel and forend are kept as offcuts
[*]A sawn-off gun is named and drawn for what was cut ("Mosin-Nagant Obrez", "Sawn-Off Double Barrel Pistol"), with its own icon
[*]Guns held like a pistol (Post-93's MP7s and P90) hang the right way up on attachment slots other mods add, such as Plysken Attachments Reborn's backpack slots, and sawn-off shotguns in modded holsters (sandbox option)
[*]9x39mm round renamed SP-5, 5.45x39mm round renamed 7N6
[/list]

[h2]M249 Light Machine Gun and belt links[/h2]
[list]
[*]M249 Light Machine Gun (the 1993 PIP gun): full auto at 750 or 1000 rounds a minute (its gas regulator), fed from its own 200-round box or, in an emergency, any of Guns of Marz's STANAG magazines (which jam twice as often), integrated bipod; found where Guns of Marz's M60 is (army zombies, army storage, heavy army trucks), with its boxes
[*]Belt links: filling the M249's 200-round box or Guns of Marz's M60 100-round box now takes one belt link per round (M27 links for the M249, M13 for the M60). Unloading a round, firing it or racking it out gives its link back: fired and racked links are thrown out of the gun (with Hot Brass they fly and land like casings, without it they drop at your feet; Ammo Maker's brass catchers catch them). A belt box found loaded counts as linked
[*]Links come in boxes (200 M27, 100 M13) in the M249 and M60 kits and in army ammunition storage, or are made at any surface from a small sheet of metal with sheet metal snips, a hammer and a metalworking punch (Make M27 5.56mm or M13 7.62mm Belt Links, Metalworking 2: 50 M27 or 40 M13 links)
[/list]

[h2]Uzi, Mini Uzi and Micro Uzi[/h2]
[list]
[*]Uzi (held like a rifle, as the MP5, its metal stock folding under the receiver), Mini Uzi and Micro Uzi (held one-handed and fitting a holster like the MP5K and MAC-10, the Micro also an ankle holster); the Mini's and Micro's stocks fold along the right side: automatic at 600, 950 and 1200 rounds a minute, or single shots
[*]One set of IMI magazines fits all three: 20, 25 and 32 rounds, and long 40 and 50-round magazines
[*]The AWC Mk9 suppressor fits all three on its 9mm SMG muzzle mount
[*]Found with Guns of Marz's short SMGs (gun stores, pawn shops, survivalists, bandits); the Uzi also with its police SMGs and on SWAT zombies, and with its criminals' guns (criminal zombies, bandit bags, pawn shops, stashes); the long magazines with its high-capacity magazines
[/list]

[h2]Steyr AUG A1[/h2]
[list]
[*]Steyr AUG A1, the green Austrian bullpup of 1977 (imported to the US until 1989): automatic at 700 rounds a minute or single shots, its integral 1.5x scope, and a front grip that folds forward under the barrel (Fold / Unfold Foregrip in the gun's menu and the firearm radial; unfolded, it steadies aim and automatic fire)
[*]Its own translucent 30 and 42-round magazines
[*]Found with Guns of Marz's imported rifles (gun stores, pawn shops, police evidence, survivalists, rifle cases), with .223 boxes; the 42-round magazine with its high-capacity magazines
[/list]

[h2]Ammo Maker (optional)[/h2]
Used when it is enabled, as in Guns of Marz. Without it the new rounds cannot be made.
[list]
[*]Recipes for every new round
[*]Soft point bullets: Make Soft Point Bullet
[*]Birdshot pellets: Pour Birdshot Pellets
[/list]
Removing Ammo Maker from an existing save deletes Ammo Maker's own items (gunpowder, casings, bullets, tools) as players log in and areas load, as for any removed mod. Guns of Tien's rounds stay.

[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
On the [b]Guns of Tien[/b] page of the sandbox settings.
[list]
[*][i]Adjusted Piercing[/i] (on by default): plain (full metal jacket) .30-06, 7.62x51mm and .308 rounds pierce in every gun that fires them, and plain .30-30 rounds (flat-nosed soft points in reality) no longer do. Off = Guns of Marz's own piercing
[*][i]Upright Guns in Modded Attachment Slots[/i] (on by default): guns held like a pistol hang upright on other mods' rifle slots, sawn-off shotguns in their holster slots. Takes effect the next time the game is loaded
[*][i]7.62x39mm US Subsonic Cycling[/i]: always rack after every shot, rack unless a PBS-1 is fitted (default), or never rack
[/list]
