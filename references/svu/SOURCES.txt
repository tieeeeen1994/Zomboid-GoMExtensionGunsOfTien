SVU (OTs-03) family - reference set, gathered 2026-11-20
==========================================================
Per-file source lines (title, page, image URL) are kept in each subfolder's own SOURCES.txt
(svu/, svu_a/, svu_as/, ots03_later/, docs/, magazines/, replicas/). contact_<folder>.png = labelled contact sheet.
The build target is the ORIGINAL semi-auto SVU (OTs-03, 1991). Everything else is kept apart.

FACTS (sources at the end of each line; [W] weapon.at.ua 2011 archive, [P] Popenker modernfirearms.net/world.guns.ru,
[RW] ru.wikipedia "Снайперская винтовка укороченная", [EW] en.wikipedia "Dragunov SVU", [WP] weaponplace.ru/svu.php
(early-1990s magazine article), [POS] MVD training poster docs/svu_mvd_poster_2048.jpg, [IM] IMFDB "SVD Dragunov",
[TS] TsKIB SOO site 2014 archive, [K] Vitaly V. Kuzmin photos on Wikimedia Commons)

SVU / OTs-03 (base, semi-automatic) - THE ONE TO BUILD
- Maker: TsKIB SOO, Tula (later a branch of KBP). Designer L. V. Bondarev [RW][WP]. Bullpup conversion of the SVD.
- Dates: work began 1975-77 for the VDV; demonstration samples shown to VDV units 1979, not adopted [W][RW][WP].
  Series production started 1991 (at the request of the "Vympel" special unit [W]; first rifles to the 106th Guards
  Airborne Division for troop trials [RW, citing Soldat Udachi 1/2001]). Adopted by the MVD in the early 1990s [RW][P].
  IMFDB: "introduced 1991, entered service three years later" (i.e. 1994) [IM]; en.wiki: design 1990, production
  1994-, first seen in the First Chechen War [EW]. Popenker: production "mid-1990s" [P].
  => existed (series production) by July 1993 in every source that gives 1991; main mod is fine.
- Users: MVD special units (OMON/SOBR), FSB; VDV trials; Belarus (unsourced); Yemen (SVU-A seen 2018) [RW][EW].
- Dimensions: length 900 mm [POS][P] (870 mm in [W]/[RW]/en.wiki, citing TsKIB; 980 mm on the 2014 TsKIB page [TS] is
  the later 620 mm-barrel OTs-03 - ignore). Barrel 520 mm, i.e. SVD barrel cut by 100 mm, gas chamber moved back [W][P].
  Weight 4.4 kg with empty magazine and PSO-1 [POS][W][P]. Poster also gives: height with open sight 229 mm, width 50 mm,
  iron sight radius 423 mm, 830 m/s, 10-round magazine, 30 rds/min practical [POS].
- Magazine: standard SVD 10-round steel magazine [W][P][RW]. (20/30-rounders were made for the SVU-A/AS; Popenker says
  apparently never in quantity.)
- Muzzle device: Bondarev's three-chamber combined brake / flash hider / "sound moderator" (cuts up to 40 % of recoil,
  adds forward weight) [W][WP]. Same cylindrical can on all variants in the photos; estimated ~145-150 mm long and
  ~40-45 mm diameter from Kuzmin's flat right side view (svu_a/kuzmin2015_svua_01.jpg, scaled to 900 mm) - ESTIMATE.
- Sights: folding diopter rear (100-1300 m drum) and hooded front post, both on folding posts; SVD-type side rail on the
  left of the receiver, moved forward and up; PSO-1 (1P43) normal, NSPU night sights possible; the scope doubles as a
  carry handle [W][WP][P].
- Furniture: SVD polymer handguards (left one notched for the forward scope mount), pistol grip near the centre of
  gravity with an enlarged guard for gloves, fixed cheek rest on the receiver cover, butt (shoulder piece) held on a
  leaf spring that rocks forward on recoil [W][WP]. "Butt, receiver, pistol grip and handguard are usually given
  camouflage paint" [W][WP] - the IMFDB SVU photo (svu/imfdb_Svu.jpg) is red-brown/black camo; Kuzmin's 1994 rifles have
  worn camo-painted WOODEN pistol grips and wooden cheek pieces.
- Bayonet: the MVD poster's delivery kit lists a bayonet (штык-нож, items 2/12) [POS]; en.wiki: "a bayonet may be fitted
  under the forestock" [EW]; weaponplace says no bayonet mount [WP]. CONFLICT - poster is primary, so the SVU took one.
- Safety: the poster lists a "предохранитель" (safety) whose flag is lowered to fire [POS].

SVU-A / OTs-03A (select-fire) - LATER, keep separate
- Developed on MVD demand: 1991 (Sytin 2012 via [RW]; [WP] says MVD asked in 1991), "developed and adopted in 1993" [W],
  "introduced in 1995" [IM]. Borderline for the July 1993 cut; most likely Post-93 or exactly 1993. Kuzmin's SVU-A
  receivers are stamped 1994 (svu_a/kuzmin2014_svua_1994_06.jpg: "1994", serial 39771).
- Same gun externally. Auto by long trigger pull; a travel-limiting selector in the trigger guard [W][P].
- Tell in photos: the right-side receiver lever (S-shaped, behind the magazine) has 3 stamped positions "ПР" / "АВ" /
  (ОД) - see svu_a/kuzmin2014_svua_1994_06.jpg and kuzmin2015_svua_01.jpg ("ПР", "АВ" readable). IMFDB: "note 3-position
  fire selector". The SVU-AS seen by "Kardell" also has a ribbed slide tab marked "О" above the trigger, right side
  (svu_as/q6_038.jpg, q6_040.jpg). A base SVU should show no "АВ" stamp (UNVERIFIED: no close-up of a confirmed base
  SVU's right side was found; the poster photos are too small to read).
- Popenker and IMFDB label the SAME right-side studio photo differently (svu_a/imfdb_SVU-A.jpg: IMFDB "SVU-A",
  modernfirearms "basic SVU with open sights raised") - not usable as a variant proof.
- Weight 4.4 kg, magazines 10/20/30, 650 rds/min cyclic [W][P].

SVU-AS / OTs-03AS (select-fire + bipod) - POST-93
- Developed and adopted 1995 after Chechnya [W]; ru.wiki also says "2001" (that is the Soldat Udachi article date) [RW].
- Bipod on a separate horizontal bar fixed to the front of the receiver under the handguard (barrel free-floated),
  telescopic legs with 4 heights (max 320 mm), swivel 90 deg either side; 5.5 kg with PSO-1 and bipod [W][P].
- Tell: the bar / folded bipod under the handguard (obvious in every photo).

LATER OTs-03 (2009+ TsKIB catalogue, expo rifles): 620 mm barrel, 980 mm, 7.62x51 option, top and side rails, DS5 day/
night sight, 5.9-6.1 kg [TS][RW]. ots03_later/ only.

HOW TO SORT PHOTOS: bipod bar under the handguard = SVU-AS. No bar + "АВ" on the right lever = SVU-A. No bar and
neither visible = SVU or SVU-A (externally identical; build geometry from either). Black polymer grip/cheek, rails or a
long barrel = later production.
