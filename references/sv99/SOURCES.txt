Izhmash / Kalashnikov SV-99 (СВ-99) .22 LR sniper rifle: references gathered 2026-11-20, before modelling.
Contact sheets: contact_sheet_green_plywood.png / _wood.png / _black_polymer.png / _civilian.png / _docs.png /
_biathlon_relatives.png. Not in git (references/ is ignored); photos belong to their sites. The gun is rare:
nearly every real photo online comes from a handful of sets (KardeN's green-plywood set, the guns.ru
"новое ложа" thread, Kalashnikov's catalogue shots, museum and expo snaps), re-posted many times.

FACTS (sources in brackets; [cg] club.guns.ru/sv99.html (2000 article, tied to the РОСТ-99 show),
[gc] guns.club/lib/oruzhie/vintovka-sv-99, [wl] weaponland.ru "Малый калибр, большая польза" (Руслан Гук,
Warspot), [ruw] ru.wikipedia СВ-99, [ao] armoury-online.ru, [gd] gundata.ru, [mf] modernfirearms.net (Popenker),
[imfdb], [kal] Kalashnikov catalogue sheet docs/y1_033.jpg, [man] manual page docs/gunsru_p1_1524938.jpg,
[gr] forum.guns.ru thread 248214 "Винтовка СВ-99 получила новое ложа" 2007-2009)
- Maker: Izhevsk Machine-Building Plant (Izhmash, now Kalashnikov Concern). Team led by Vladimir Susloparov
  (sport/hunting weapons bureau), asked informally by the Udmurt FSB [gr, cg, gc]. Action from the BI-7-2
  biathlon rifle (Dragunov's 1979 toggle design), magazine/stock ideas from the Sobol carbine [wl, gr].
- Years: designed late 1990s, shown 1999-2000 (name = 1999) [cg, gc, armedman]; adopted by FSB 2004, MVD 2005,
  Internal Troops 2007 [wl]; still in Kalashnikov's catalogue (black polymer) [kal]. All after July 1993 ->
  Post-93 add-on.
- Users: FSB, MVD OMON / SOBR (now Rosgvardia) [wl, ruw, gc]; Armenian police (2011 decree) [ruw].
- Calibre .22 LR (5.6 mm rimfire). Barrel 350 mm (351 mm [gc]; rifled 334 mm), 6 right-hand grooves,
  1:420 mm, cold-forged, not chromed, free-floated [cg, gc, kal]. Muzzle thread for the suppressor
  "Сп25,5х1,5" (M25.5x1.5) on a muzzle bush [gc; guns.ru civilian thread 2015 says the same].
- Length 1030 mm with butt and suppressor [kal, gc, ruw]; 1000 mm in the 2000 article and [mf]/[imfdb] (early
  gun or without suppressor, unclear); 720 mm without butt [wl, armedman]; barrelled action + forend without
  butt and suppressor 520 mm [cg]. 890 mm without suppressor and height 240 / width 85 mm come from a fan-made
  "technical data" card (docs/y3_022.jpg, by Ricardo Torres; it also gives a wrong 993 m/s velocity):
  unverified. Height 195 mm [ao].
- Weight: 3.4 kg, 4.5 kg with suppressor [kal]; 4.5 kg with scope and suppressor [man]; 3.75 kg without scope
  [cg, mf, gd]; ru-wiki 3.35 kg without scope and suppressor, 3.75 kg with the standard scope. Sources disagree.
- Suppressor: detachable baffle can, about 150 mm long [gc], screwed on; knurled grip band at its rear end
  (photos). Not quick-detach (guns.ru users complain). Velocity 345 m/s [ruw, armedman].
- Magazines: detachable plastic box, 5 rounds standard, 10 optional [cg, gc, kal, man]. Two spare magazines in
  the butt (under a flip cover, bottom rear of the butt) [ao, ruw, gc]. No dedicated magazine photo found; small
  ones show in the kit shots (green_plywood/y1_004, y2_005, gunsru_p1_1524531). Whether they are BI-7
  magazines is unverified.
- Action: straight-pull toggle (knee-joint) bolt from the BI-7-2: the lever lies along the RIGHT side of the
  receiver and swings back/forward with finger and thumb [cg, gc, mf]. Left-hand lever to order [wl]
  (a guns.ru user saw left-lever photos, 2009). Cylindrical receiver. Trigger adjustable 0.5-1.0 kg, two-stage.
  Safety: push-button at the front of the trigger guard, blocks the trigger [gc, ruw, ao].
- Sights: no iron sights. Early guns: dovetail on the receiver, PO 4x34 scope; serial guns from 2007: integral
  Weaver/Picatinny rail, PO 3.5x17.5P scope (Minsk Vavilov plant) [gr, gc, wl, gd].
- Stock: two-part, butt detaches with one button at the wrist; a pistol grip (wooden on early guns) can replace
  it; symmetrical (ambidextrous). Butt: LOP +20 mm, cheek rest up 7.5 / fore-aft 6 mm, butt plate +-30 mm
  [cg, gd]; folding rear monopod in the butt (from 2007) [gr, armedman]. Forend rail/slot for a height-adjustable
  folding bipod [cg, ruw]. Sling swivel on the left of the forend (black photos). Carried in a hard case or a
  Cordura soft case [gr] (docs/zonwar_i969-970).

VARIANTS (how to tell them apart; all are the same barrelled action)
1. Early wood (about 1999-2006) [ruw: varnished wood; "Мастер-ружьё" 2000/2]: reddish-brown lacquered
   (laminated-looking) wood, thumbhole butt with a separate raised cheek board and a white-spacer hooked
   butt plate, slim round-bottomed forend, dovetail scope base, PO4x34 or a hunting 4x scope, bipod under the
   forend. Wooden pistol grip shown in wood/y1_010. Photos: wood/. Worth a variant at most; the shape differs
   from the later stocks (no monopod, different butt).
2. Green plywood (2007-2009 serial) [ruw, ao, gr 25-9-2007]: dark green laminated aviation plywood "like the
   SV-98": SV-98-style skeleton butt with two rectangular holes and a thumbhole, adjustable cheek and hooked
   butt plate, retractable rear monopod, stippled grip, Picatinny/Weaver on the receiver, PO3.5x17.5P scope,
   redesigned suppressor. The 2009 factory photos (green_plywood/gunsru_p2_19464xx, b2_001/003) look
   grey-green. Photos: green_plywood/ (best coverage of every angle: KardeN's set). The most recognisable
   SV-99; build this one.
3. Black polymer (2009 to now; shown at РОСТ/Interpolitex 2009) [ruw, gr p3, kal]: black plastic stock with a
   straight-topped open skeleton butt (one large hole), box-shaped cheek riser on top, hooked (horned) butt
   plate, rear monopod knob under the butt, slab-sided forend with left sling swivel; receiver marked "СВ-99"
   and the Izhmash logo on the left; silver toggle lever on the right; knurled suppressor. Current catalogue gun.
   Photos: black_polymer/ (official both-side views at 2120 px). Worth building as its own gun or a stock
   variant (very different silhouette from the green one).
4. Civilian relatives (not SV-99s): BI-7-2KO hunting carbine in SV-99-style stocks without suppressor: camo
   stock at a 2012 show (civilian/gunsru_civ_6625403, label "БИ-7-2КО"), grey stock at Arms & Hunting 2017
   (civilian/y1_016, y1_027: receiver marked "БИАТЛОН-7-2-КО", Picatinny), a black one from a guns.ru "TV-1
   family" thread (civilian/y1_050), and an owner's black prototype (civilian/gunsru_civ_5526257, unverified).
   Warfiles/TV1 (2017) called it a "civilian SV-99". Not worth a separate gun; at most a no-suppressor variant.
   Many guns.ru sale posts show aftermarket "SV-99-type" plywood stocks for BI-7-2 / Sobol: those were dropped.
