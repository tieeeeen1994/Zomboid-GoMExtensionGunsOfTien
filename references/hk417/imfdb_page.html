Heckler & Koch HK417

The HK417 is a battle rifle variant of the HK416, chambering the 7.62x51mm NATO round. The 13" HK417 A2 was adopted in interim by the Bundeswehr (before the G28 was ready) under the designation G27P.

Specifications

(2006 - Present)
(2013 - Present (A2))

  • Type: Battle Rifle / Sniper Rifle
  • Caliber(s): 7.62x51mm NATO
  • Weight: 8.5 lbs (3.9 kg), 8.9 lbs (4.1 kg), 9.3 lbs (4.2 kg)
  • Length: 34.8 in (88.5 cm), 38.8 in (98.5 cm), 42.7 in (108.5 cm)
  • Barrel Length: 12 in (30.5 cm), 16 in (40.6 cm), 20 in (50.8 cm)
  • Feed System: 10, 20 round box magazine, 50 round drum magazine
  • Fire Modes: Semi-Auto/Full-Auto

Heckler & Koch G28

The G28, known in Germany as the HK241, is a military version of the MR308 adopted by the German Army as a designated marksman rifle. A variation of this rifle was adopted by the US Military as the M110A1 CSASS / SDMR in 2016.
