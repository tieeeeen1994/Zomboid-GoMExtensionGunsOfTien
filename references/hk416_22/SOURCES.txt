HK416 .22 LR pistols: references gathered 2026-11-13, before modelling (user: "WHY NOT BOTH???", so both
generations). Contact sheets: contact_sheet_gen1.png / _gen2.png / _magazines.png / _docs.png. Not in git
(references/ is ignored); photos belong to their sites.

GEN 1 = Walther HK416 .22 Pistol (Carl Walther under licence, imported by Walther Arms / Umarex USA, about
2009/10 to 2018; 5780303 = 20 rds, 578030310 = 10 rds): 9" (229 mm) barrel inside a long round shroud
that looks like a can (the .22 barrel is a liner in a shroud), short quad rail (RIS) with a top rail
continuous with the receiver's, H&K-style front sight on the rail's front and H&K-style rotary diopter
rear, H&K grip, no buffer tube (the receiver ends in a round cap), 17.2" (437 mm), 5.0 lb (2.27 kg) without
magazine, 10 / 20 rounds, muzzle thread M8x0.75 (Small Arms Review, Feb 2011). Black.
GEN 2 = H&K USA HK416 .22 LR Pistol (Umarex, Germany; H&K USA relaunch 2019; 81000403 = 20 rds,
81000404 = 10 rds, FDE / two-tone / grey / bronze variants 81000530 / 531 / 598 / 976 ...): HK416 A5-style
M-LOK handguard (Mil-STD-1913 top), polymer flip-up sights, 4-prong flash hider, H&K grip, functional dust
cover, no buffer tube. H&K USA sheet (docs/bing_217): 425 mm long, barrel 217 mm (8.5", 1/2x28), width
63.8 mm, height 272 mm, 2050 g without magazine, 10 / 20 / 30 rounds, rear aperture height 33 mm, rear
aperture width 54.2 mm, front sight width 1.8 mm. H&K GmbH card (docs/bing_005): sight radius 304.6 mm, 326
m/s, 137 J, "polymer flip-up, the rear fully adjustable", 1 turn in 13.75", no stock (optional brace).
