Izhmash / Kalashnikov SV-98 (6V10) bolt-action sniper rifle: references gathered 2026-11-20, before modelling.
Contact sheets: contact_sheet_<folder>.png (sv98, sv98_alu, sv98m, sv338, record, magazines, docs, airsoft).
Not in git (references/ is ignored); photos belong to their sites. Per-file sources are merged below (folder/file,
page title, page URL and image URL); each folder also keeps its own SOURCES.txt with the same lines.

ORIGIN. Designed by a team under Vladimir D. Stronskiy at Izhmash (Izhevsk) in 1998-2000 (ru.wikipedia), from the
"Record-CISM" 300 m full-bore target rifle (Record family, 1972 lineage); the SV-98 adds a detachable 10-round
magazine (ru.wikipedia; Kalashnikov 2017 product page: "developed on the basis of the Record-CISM"). Adopted 2003 by
special-operations units as part of the 6S11 sniper system (rifle 6V10 + 7N14 round) (en.wikipedia; ru.wikipedia
infobox gives service from 2003, series production from 2004). Shown publicly at the "First All-Russian spetsnaz
rally" in Izhevsk in January (c. 2000; club.guns.ru article, written alongside ROST-99 / SV-99 pages) already in the
green stock with carry handle, PKS-07 scope and suppressor, so the plywood-stock rifle existed by 2000.
Bolt: rotating, three symmetrical front lugs, cadmium-plated (grey) bolt body, cocking indicator at the rear of the
bolt, handle on the RIGHT with a ball knob, angled down. Safety: lever ("flag") on the right rear of the receiver,
just behind the bolt handle, moved forward/back; locks trigger, sear and bolt. Magazine catch: large button; the
magazine slides straight up along a vertical guide rod in the well (no SVD-style rock-in) (club.guns.ru,
maksimov.su). Barrel: cold-hammer forged, free-floated, 650 mm, 4 grooves RH, twist 320 mm; early guns not
chrome-lined (club.guns.ru c.2000), the 2017-18 Kalashnikov pages say chromed bore and chamber.

1) SV-98, plywood (laminated veneer) stock, 7.62x54R. The classic look: green-stained laminated "aviation plywood"
   thumbhole stock with long oval grooves along the forend, stippled pistol grip, skeleton butt with two windows,
   adjustable comb (cheek piece) and rubber butt plate adjustable for length, height and cant; folding bipod in a
   slot under the forend and a fold-down rear monopod in the butt; detachable carry handle bolted to the stock
   (protects the scope); anti-mirage strap. Muzzle: threaded, with a conical "birdcage" muzzle device / thread
   protector sleeve, or the factory sound suppressor (TGP-V); the front sight (hooded post) sits on a band near the
   muzzle, rear sight is a drum (barrel-type) sight on the receiver graduated to 600 m in 100 m steps (maksimov.su).
   Optic rail on top of the receiver (a slotted rail in close-ups, e.g. sv98/commons_SV-98-21; the c.2000 text calls
   it an "upper mounting plank"; whether early guns had a true Picatinny is unverified). Standard optics PKS-07 (7x)
   early, 1P69 "Hyperon" 3-10x42 later. Colour: green stock (shades vary from dark bottle green to bright teal; c.2000
   guns darker), black metal. A fibreglass-reinforced polymer stock was also offered (en.wikipedia; sv98/ya_084.png
   shows a black stock, unverified which).
   Specs (Kalashnikov product page 2017, archived; same table in docs/ya_027.png): 7.62x54R, 10 rds, length 1200 mm
   without suppressor / 1375 with, also "overall length 1270" (same figure as club.guns.ru c.2000; probably stock
   at full length or with muzzle device; unclear), height 300, width 200, barrel 650, mass 5.8 kg without optic and
   suppressor (5.5 "without cartridges"; club.guns.ru 6.2; ru.wikipedia 6.2), 7.8 kg with optic and suppressor,
   820 m/s, iron sights 600 m, optic 1000 m, trigger 1.0-1.5 kgf adjustable, stock length adjustment 20 mm, comb
   +-30 mm vertical / +-7 mm horizontal (club.guns.ru c.2000: butt plate up/down 30, left/right 7; cheek +9/-6
   vertical, +4 horizontal; length 20; trigger 1.5-2.0 kgf).

2) SV-98 with aluminium stock (no designation change, still 6V10). Prototypes 2013-2014 (Eurosatory 2014: skeleton
   stock, black plastic adjustable butt); series production for the MoD from 2016: B95 aluminium-alloy chassis,
   side-FOLDING skeleton butt with adjustable comb, rubber butt plate and rear monopod, separate polymer pistol grip
   (sport style), Picatinny rail on the receiver, short forend with holes / short rail segments, same 10-round
   polymer magazine, same muzzle threads / suppressor. Mostly black; tan/FDE and olive shown at expos. A version with
   a detachable (non-folding) butt was made in small numbers for other agencies and on a few civilian Records.
   Sources: Kalashnikov magazine, M. Degtyarev, 6 Aug 2024 (kalashnikov.ru "Концерн Калашников модернизирует винтовку
   СВ-98 (СВ-98М)"); Kalashnikov 2018 product card (5.8 kg, 1200 mm, aluminium stock, Picatinny). Note: IMFDB and
   English sources call this the "SV-98M"; Degtyarev says that is wrong (the MoD rifles stayed SV-98) and that the
   aluminium rifles stayed in depots until 2022, so plywood rifles were the ones in troop use before that. Two
   photos from May 2026 (sv98_alu/yb_099, yb_119; rg.ru "SVCh, SV-98 and SVDS reworked after SVO experience") show it
   still current.

3) SV-98M (the real one). Kalashnikov initiative, first prototype 2023, shown at Army-2024 as a civilian-certified
   "Record" prototype, not adopted as of the 2024 articles. Chassis layout: complex receiver with an integral
   SVD-type magazine well (takes SVD magazines; a polymer-bodied SVD-type magazine made for it), mount for any
   forend at the receiver front (the prototype has a long M-LOK tube with top rail), folding-stock hinge at the
   receiver rear, safety moved to a thumb lever on the RIGHT side of the receiver, mag catch in the trigger guard.
   2023 prototype: SV-98-aluminium-style folding stock, experimental asymmetric muzzle brake (DTK), large
   suppressor (PMS) on a plain M18x1 thread (the SV-98's muzzle seat is conical). Army-2024 version: slotted flash
   hider, new folding adjustable stock, polymer forend cover, polymer SVD-type magazine. Stainless forged barrel
   trialled. Sources: Kalashnikov magazine 6 Aug 2024 (above) and 14 Aug 2024 ("SV-98M at Army 2024").

4) 7.62x51 / .308: SV-98 offered in 7.62 NATO for export (en.wikipedia; club.guns.ru lists 7.62x54 and .308 Win);
   civilian Record-308 too. No photo shows an external difference (the magazine for the rimless round is not
   pictured separately); treat as a calibre option, not a separate model.

5) .338 Lapua: Izhmash made a .338 LM prototype in the 2000s (ru.wikipedia) and the SV-338 / SV-338M / SV-338M1
   (en.wikipedia: scaled-up magnum actions, otherwise like the SV-98; no dimensions in any source found). Only
   sv338/yb_081 (yaplakal, labelled CB-338, CB-338M, CB-338M1) shows the family: SV-338 = SV-98-like green stock,
   longer barrel and brake; -338M = green stock with carry handle; -338M1 = black chassis with folding stock, like
   the aluminium SV-98. Other sv338/ photos are attributed by their page titles only (red-bear.ru, guns.ru "SV-98M-338"
   thread); some may be the aluminium SV-98 or the unrelated Kalashnikov VSV-338 (Army-2016 prototype, 1100 mm,
   5.5 kg, 5 rds per modernfirearms.net), so treat sv338/ as unverified. Civilian Record-338 (KO-13 family):
   barrel 690 mm, 6.4 kg (sport stock) / 5.6 kg (hunting stock) (Izhmash 2010 page, archived); TFB 2010: 5 rds,
   27" barrel, 14 lb.

6) Record family (record/): Record-CISM / Record-308-CISM target rifles: 650 mm barrel, 10 rds, 5.5 kg without
   optic, 1220 x 250 x 100 mm, diopter or iron + optical sights, comb 15 / 8 mm, butt plate 30 / 7 mm (Izhmash
   2008 page, archived). Photos (IWA 2007, record/yb_043, yb_053) show the Record-6-CISM as a classic wooden
   (brown) target stock with diopter sight, NOT the SV-98 thumbhole shape; the wooden Record-338 does have the
   SV-98 shape. Record-1 / Record-2 (700 / 760 mm barrels) are older wooden target rifles. The civilian KO-13
   "Record" (low-rate from 2010 per ru.wikipedia, no suppressor mount; ads show 2007 rifles) is externally an SV-98
   (green plywood stock, carry handle), so record/ civilian photos (guns.ru sale ads) are usable as SV-98 references
   too, some with natural-wood or aluminium stocks.

Magazine (magazines/): own 10-round double-stack, glass-fibre-reinforced nylon with metal inserts (feed lips,
   catch), roughly rectangular, ribbed side panels, round boss low on the left side, short guide channel at the
   front for the well's guide rod. Not interchangeable with SVD (until the SV-98M). ru3_063/065/070/075 and the
   S&T titles are AIRSOFT replica magazines (shape copies, 1250 px product shots); maks_*, ru_024, ru_041, ru3_032,
   ru3_069, ru3_086/088/089/091, yb_121-144 are real ones (guns.ru sale ads, maksimov.su).

Variants existing by 2000: only the plywood-stock SV-98 (7.62x54R; .308 offered), with PKS-07, carry handle,
   suppressor / muzzle sleeve. The aluminium stock is 2013+ (series 2016), the SV-98M 2023+, SV-338 2000s prototypes.
