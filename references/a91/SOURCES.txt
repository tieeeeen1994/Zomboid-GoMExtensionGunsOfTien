Text: Small Arms Review (the GP-97 "an inverted GP-95", its trigger against the forward grip, "offset slightly to
the right"; charging handle at the left front of the handle; the selector "similar in shape and function to that
of Kalashnikov weapons, but in a different location"); Modern Firearms (selector on the right of the receiver,
behind the trigger guard; the launcher integral, its trigger on the forward grip); bstudy.net and zonwar.ru (cases
leave forward on the right over the pistol grip through a channel along the barrel; a folding sight for the
launcher at the front). So the plate with red marks on the receiver's right is the SELECTOR, not a case deflector
(the A-91M's notes call it that).
