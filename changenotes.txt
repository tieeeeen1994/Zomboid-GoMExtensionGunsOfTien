3.8.0

RPK and RPK-74
- New: the RPK (7.62x39mm) and RPK-74 (5.45x39mm), Kalashnikov's squad machine guns, built on Guns of Marz's AK-47 and AK-74: long heavy barrel, club-foot stock and a folding bipod (deployed, it steadies aim and automatic fire), automatic at 600 rounds a minute or single shots
- The RPK takes its own 40-round magazine and 75-round drum and Guns of Marz's AK-47 magazines. The 40 and the drum fit Guns of Marz's AK-47 too
- The RPK-74 takes Guns of Marz's AK-74 magazines (its Bakelite 45 is the RPK-74's own)
- Both take Guns of Marz's AK mount and its optics, its AK muzzle mount and the PBS-1. With the PBS-1, the US Subsonic round cycles the RPK as it does the AKs
- Found where Guns of Marz's AK-47 and AK-74 are (drug labs, police evidence, survivalists, bandits, late gun finds). The 40 and the drum also come with its high-capacity AK magazines

RPD
- New: the RPD (7.62x39mm), Degtyaryov's belt-fed squad machine gun as first made: its 100-round belt drum under the gun, automatic only at 700 rounds a minute, a folding bipod, and an operating handle that moves back and forth with every shot
- The US Subsonic round needs racking after every shot in it (no PBS-1 fits)
- Found with the RPKs

Belt links
- New sandbox option "Belt-Fed Guns Need Links" (on by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it off). Off: the M249's 200-round box and Guns of Marz's M60 box load like any magazine, no link boxes are found, and the link recipes are hidden. Links already in the world stay. A change takes effect the next time the game is loaded

Fixes
- The M249's and Guns of Marz's M60's tooltips no longer list nine "Belt Round" rows for the rounds shown on the belt
- Reloading the M249 with a STANAG magazine shows the magazine in the hand at the gun's size again
