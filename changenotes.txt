3.13.0

Steyr TMP
- New: Steyr's polymer machine pistol of 1992, held one-handed like Guns of Marz's MP5K and MAC-10 and fitting a holster, its front grip moulded in. Automatic at 900 rounds a minute, or single shots
- Its own 15 and 30-round magazines (the 15 flush with the grip)
- The AWC Mk9 suppressor fits it on the 9mm SMG muzzle mount
- Guns of Marz's optional Picatinny rails on top (its red dots), left (lights) and right (lasers)
- Found with Guns of Marz's short SMGs

SR-25
- New: Knight's SR-25 Match of 1990, Eugene Stoner's 7.62x51mm sniper rifle on the AR-10 pattern, semi-automatic, with a 24-inch match barrel in a free-float tube
- Its own 3.5-10x scope and a folding bipod built in. Deployed from the gun's menu, the bipod steadies aim and rests the rifle on low walls and tables
- Knight's 20-round magazine; with the Post-93 add-on, Magpul's PMAGs and D-50 drum fit it too
- Found where Guns of Marz's PSG-1 is, with its magazines and boxes

Mk 11 Mod 0 (Post-93 add-on)
- New: the SR-25 as US special forces adopted it in 2000: a 20-inch barrel and Knight's rail handguard with rails on all four sides for Guns of Marz's optics (its sniper scopes need no mount), foregrips, bipods, lights and lasers. Semi-automatic
- Takes the SR-25's 20-round magazine, Magpul's PMAGs and D-50 drum, and the SOCOM762-RC suppressor on its muzzle mount
- Found where the SCAR-H is, mostly with Knight's magazine

OTs-14 Groza (Post-93 add-on)
- New: four of TsKIB SOO's Groza bullpups of 1994, on the AKS-74U's mechanism, automatic at 700 rounds a minute
- Groza-1: 7.62x39mm with the long barrel. Takes Guns of Marz's AK-47 magazines and the RPK's 40 and drum, its AK muzzle mount and PBS-1 (with the PBS-1 fitted, 7.62x39 US Subsonic cycles it without racking)
- Groza-1 with GP-25: the same rifle with a GP-25 grenade launcher. Switch to it and back from Gunworks' radial menu, as with Guns of Marz's M16A2 with M203. It fires Guns of Marz's 40mm grenades, one at a time, and leaves no case to pull out
- Groza-2 Prototype: the compact 5.45x39mm one, held one-handed like the MP5K. Takes Guns of Marz's AK-74 magazines
- Groza-4: 9x39mm, its suppressor built in like Guns of Marz's AS VAL's, a little louder than the AS VAL. Takes Guns of Marz's AS VAL magazines
- All four take Guns of Marz's Picatinny rail on the carrying handle, and every Guns of Marz optic on it
- Found where Guns of Marz's late guns are found, each with its magazines and boxes

Fix
- Loot: since Guns of Marz's update of November 10, this mod's guns were found with only one magazine and one box (Guns of Marz changed how its loot spawners count items). They come with their full kits again, up to as many magazines and boxes as before

Change
- The SCAR-H's muzzle mount is now the "7.62x51mm Family Muzzle Mounting Device": the SCAR-H, the MDRx .308 and the Mk 11 take it
- The MDRx .308 also takes the SR-25's 20-round magazine
