Post-93 loot reworked: its guns are found in the places that fit them, not only late in homes and bags.

Until now most Post-93 guns shared Guns of Marz's spawner for its post-1993 guns, which picks one of about 20 guns and only turns up in late-game homes and survivor or bandit bags. Each gun now joins the Guns of Marz spawner for its kind of gun, packed the same way, and turns up wherever Guns of Marz's guns of that kind do:
- SWAT submachine guns (the MP7s, P90 TR, UMPs, Vector .45 and 9mm, AUG Para), with Guns of Marz's MP5s: SWAT vehicles, police bags, prison riot storage, and on SWAT zombies
- Modern military rifles (the SCARs, HK416, HK417s, Mk 11), with Guns of Marz's M16s and M14: army storage, the bunker, army trucks and packs, military rifle cases, late homes and late bandit bags, and on army zombies; the HK416 and SCAR-L also as SWAT and police patrol rifles, the Mk 11 and HK417 Marksman also with army snipers in ghillie suits
- Civilian rifles (the MDRx .223 and .308, AUG A3 and HBAR, PS90, Vector CRB, HK416 .22), with Guns of Marz's imported rifles and the AUG A1: gun stores, pawn shops, police evidence, survivalists' vehicles, rifle cases, late homes, and on survivalist zombies
- Pistols (the Five-seveN, Vector SDP), with Guns of Marz's imported pistols: gun stores, pawn shops, police evidence, drug labs, stashes, bandits' and luxury cars
- Russian guns (the Grozas, SV-98, SV-99), with Guns of Marz's AKs and SVD: drug labs, police evidence, stashes, survivalists' and bandits' vehicles, late homes and safehouses; with Undead Survivors the Grozas also with its mercenaries and the SV-98 and SV-99 with its mercenary snipers
SWAT gun storage, where Guns of Marz placed no rifles or submachine guns of its own, now holds SWAT submachine guns and carbines, Guns of Marz's MP5s and CAR-15s among them. Police stations' gun storage, police cars and police zombies now carry patrol rifles (Guns of Marz's CAR-15s, the HK416 and SCAR-L), ordinary pawn shops sell the civilian rifles too, and army snipers in ghillie suits can carry the Mk 11 or HK417 Marksman.
Guns of Marz's post-1993 spawner holds only its own five guns again (G36, Benelli M4, AA-12, Rhino, MP-412 REX), so they are found more often.
The UCP, a prototype that was never sold, no longer turns up in survivor and bandit bags; police and SWAT still carry it.
New sandbox option Extend Loot Areas (Guns of Tien page, off by default): Zombies carry guns far more often, and every gun, Guns of Marz's included, turns up in more places that bend reality but still make sense in game. Most police officers, soldiers, bandits, hunters, park rangers, survivalists and militia carry a gun (the game arms about one police zombie in four and one soldier or hunter in fifty), ghillie snipers always their rifle. Guns of Marz's post-1993 guns reach gun stores, SWAT armouries and police and civilian zombies, its short submachine guns shoulder holsters and drug labs, its imported pistols holsters, its Russian rifles criminals and survivalists, its M79 and M60 more soldiers, its Double Deuce hunters and its cowboy rifles gun stores and ranger stations. With the Post-93 add-on, regular police also carry submachine guns, criminals Grozas, UMP45s and Vector 9mms, survivalists SCAR-Ls, HK416s and Groza-1s, civilians Five-seveNs and Vector SDPs, and the Post-93 civilian rifles can take the place of the game's own semi-automatic rifle wherever it spawns, homes included (only while Guns of Marz's vanilla weapon replacement is on). Guns of Marz's own guns get rarer in the spawners other guns join.
Applies to containers and zombies that have not been looted or spawned yet.
