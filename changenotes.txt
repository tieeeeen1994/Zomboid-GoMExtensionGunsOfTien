Bolts and charging handles
- Every gun's charging handle except the SCARs' now stays forward while firing and moves only when the gun is racked, as on the real guns (the SCAR's rides with its bolt carrier)
- What moves on each shot, and stays back on an empty gun, is the bolt, seen through the ejection port: on the right of the Uzis, MP7s, UMPs and AUGs, under the P90 and the M249
- The SCAR-L and SCAR-H are now held and reloaded like Guns of Marz's BAR, racked on the left where the SCAR's charging handle is

Attachment slots
- The MP7s and P90 now hang the right way up on attachment slots added by other mods (such as Plysken Attachments Reborn's backpack slots), not only on the back slot; sawn-off shotguns likewise in modded holsters. New sandbox option, on by default: Upright Guns in Modded Attachment Slots

Fixes
- No more error in the log when an NPC from a mod such as A-Life equips a gun
