3.16.0

Fixed bayonets
- A gun with a bayonet fitted, or the SKS with its folding bayonet, now has Fix Bayonet: on its own key (Fix / Unfix Bayonet in the controls, Z by default), in the firearm radial and in the gun's menu. The gun becomes a spear until you choose Unfix Bayonet (key or menu), which gives it back with its ammunition, magazine, parts and hotbar slot
- Fixed, it cannot fire. It stabs with Guns of Marz's bayonet stats and trains Spear, Strength and Fitness, now also on a server, where bayonet stabs gave no experience before
- Stabbing wears the bayonet at its own rate. A worn-out bayonet comes off broken
- A shove or a sprint no longer stabs while a bayonet is fitted
- New sandbox option Fixed Bayonets (on by default). Off brings back Guns of Marz's own bayonet. It applies at once, and guns already fixed can still be unfixed

HK416 and HK417 (Post-93 add-on)
- A foregrip and a bipod fitted together no longer overlap under the handguard
