Two new calibres, the 7.62x25mm Tokarev and the 9x18mm Makarov, with four guns for them, 22 new special loads (two of them incendiary), and in the Post-93 add-on KBP's two A-91M bullpups with their grenade launchers (3.25.0).

Calibres
- 7.62x25mm Tokarev, the round of the Tokarev pistols and Soviet submachine guns: boxes of 50, cartons and crates, with a Steel Core load (armour piercing, up to 5 targets) and a heavy Subsonic load. Its boxes carry the SAKER falcon.
- 9x18mm Makarov, the Soviet police and army pistol round: boxes of 50, cartons and crates, under the TUSKER boar.
- New guns for it: the Tokarev TT-33, the Soviet service pistol, with its 8-round magazine, and the PPSh-41 submachine gun with its 71-round drum and 35-round box (about 900 rounds a minute, single shots on its selector). Both are found with Guns of Marz's wartime guns, the pistol with its imported pistols too, with 7.62x25mm boxes.
- New guns for the 9x18mm: the Skorpion vz. 82 machine pistol, held one-handed and holstered like the MP5K, with a folding wire stock and straight magazines of 12, 24 and 30 rounds (about 850 rounds a minute, single shots on its selector); and in the Post-93 add-on the PP-91 Kedr (1994), the Russian police submachine gun, held the same way, with 20 and 30-round magazines and a stock that folds over the top (1000 rounds a minute). Both are smuggled guns, found with Guns of Marz's Russian guns (drug labs, stashes, police evidence), with 9x18mm boxes; with Extend Loot Areas on they are with the short submachine guns instead (gun stores, pawn shops, shoulder holsters).
- 7.62x25mm and 9x18mm boxes and cartons are found in gun stores beside the 9mm ones; with Extend Loot Areas on, wherever 9mm boxes are.

Special loads (every calibre checked for real loads that play differently; like the others, made with Ammo Maker, never found as loot)
- 7.62x51mm M61 and .30-06 Springfield M2 armour piercing rounds (up to 5 targets).
- 5.45x39mm 7U1 subsonic and 7N10 (up to 4 targets).
- Every special load rebalanced on Guns of Marz's own 5.56 numbers: armour piercing x0.85 damage and up to 5 targets for a calibre's best penetrator (lesser ones fewer targets, more damage), hollow and soft points x1.2, overpressure (+P+, hyper velocity, PMM) x1.5 with 2 targets, +P half way, rifle subsonics x0.4.
- .38 Special +P and hollow point, .357 Magnum hollow point, .44 Magnum hollow point, and the .44 Special: a milder round every .44 Magnum gun fires.
- 12 gauge flechettes: further and tighter than buckshot, each dart lighter. Ammo Maker: Make Flechettes from nails.
- 7.62x54mmR B-32 armour piercing incendiary: 1 hit in 4 sets a zombie on fire for 3 seconds, each hit starting it again, and 4 burns kill. The fire stays on the zombie: it never lights the ground it walks over or where it falls. Any other fire (a Molotov, burning ground) takes over and burns as usual.
- The plain 7.62x54mmR now pierces a second target with Realistic Piercing, as the 7.62x51 does.

Post-93 add-on
- KBP A-91M 39, a 7.62x39mm bullpup with a 40mm grenade launcher under the barrel: Guns of Marz's AK magazines, automatic or single shots, switch to the launcher with Gunworks' underbarrel control (Guns of Marz's 40mm rounds), Guns of Marz's rail on the carrying handle for optics, AK muzzle devices and the PBS-1 suppressor.
- A-91M 54 Prototype, a battle rifle on the first A-91M's body with the 40mm grenade launcher over the barrel: 7.62x54mmR (a chambering the real gun never had) on the SV-98's magazines, its 10 and a 20-round one made for this rifle, a longer and heavier barrel, automatic or single shots at 600 rounds a minute, two grips joined by a frame, the launcher's own trigger under the muzzle and its sight leaf raised while the launcher is in use; Guns of Marz's rail on the carrying handle for optics, no muzzle parts.
- SV-98 20Rds 7.62x54mm Magazine: a 20-round magazine for the A-91M 54 Prototype that the SV-98 takes too; found with the prototype and with Guns of Marz's high-capacity Russian magazines.
- Later loads (Ammo Maker, never loot): 7.62x25mm Tokarev hollow point, 9x18mm Makarov PMM and RG028 AP, 9x19mm 7N21 AP, 5.45x39mm 7N22 AP, 7.62x39mm hollow point, 7.62x54mmR soft point and 7N13 AP, 9x39mm PAB-9 AP, .30-30 FTX, .50 AE hollow point, 4.6x30mm DM21.
- 12 gauge Dragon's Breath: almost no hitting power, but 1 pellet hit in 5 sets a zombie on fire for 3 seconds (4 burns kill; the fire stays on the zombie).
- Both are found with the Grozas: Russian rifle spawns, mercenaries and (with Extend Loot Areas) criminals.
