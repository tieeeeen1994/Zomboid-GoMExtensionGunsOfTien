No more animations of our own
- Every gun of this mod and Post-93 is now held and reloaded with Guns of Marz's own animations, those of the closest Guns of Marz gun: Uzi and UMPs like the MP5; Mini Uzi, Micro Uzi, MP7s and P90 like the MP5K; Five-seveN and UCP like Guns of Marz's pistols; all AUGs like the FAMAS; SCAR-L like the FNC; SCAR-H like the FAL; M249 like the M60
- The short SMG stance and the bullpup stance are gone. The Uzi is held as a rifle again, and the Mini Uzi and MP7s are one-handed again, like the MP5K
- Guns of Marz's MP5s, MP5K and FAMAS are back on Guns of Marz's own holds and reloads
- No longer clashes with mods that change the character's animations, and the download is about 65 MB smaller

Charging handles
- The Uzis, MP7s, P90, UMPs and M249 now move their charging handle as Guns of Marz's guns do: back on each shot, held back when empty, and moving with the rack
- Remodelled from photos: the MP7's T-handle, the P90's handles and their steel tracks, the slot in the Uzis' top cover, and the M249's handle as a separate part

Guns from older saves keep working; an M249 gets its handle back as soon as it is equipped. Servers need a restart to download the update.
