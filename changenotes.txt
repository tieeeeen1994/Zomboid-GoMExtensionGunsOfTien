Post-93 loot reworked: its guns are found more often, in the places that fit them, without making Guns of Marz's own guns rarer.

Until now most Post-93 guns were packed into Guns of Marz's spawner for its post-1993 guns, which shares one slot among about 20 guns and only turns up in late-game homes and survivor or bandit bags; the SMGs and the military rifles also crowded Guns of Marz's police SMG and army rifle spawners. Post-93 now has five loot spawners of its own, each placed where Guns of Marz places its spawner for the same kind of gun, at the same rate (scaled by Guns of Marz's loot multiplier):
- SWAT submachine guns (MP7s, P90 TR, UMPs, Vector .45 and 9mm, AUG Para): SWAT storage and vehicles, police bags, prison riot storage, and on SWAT zombies
- Modern military rifles (SCARs, HK416, HK417s, Mk 11): army storage, the bunker, army trucks and packs, military rifle cases, late bandit bags, and on army zombies
- Civilian rifles (MDRx .223 and .308, AUG A3 and HBAR, PS90, Vector CRB, HK416 .22, and Guns of Marz's Benelli M4): gun stores, pawn shops, police evidence, survivalists' vehicles, rifle cases, late homes and late survivor and bandit bags
- Pistols (Five-seveN, Vector SDP, and Guns of Marz's Rhino): gun stores, pawn shops, police evidence, drug labs, stashes, bandits' and luxury cars, late survivor and bandit bags
- Russian guns (Grozas, SV-98, SV-99, and Guns of Marz's MP-412 REX): drug labs, police evidence, survivalists' and bandits' vehicles, late homes, and mercenary zombies with Undead Survivors
The Five-seveN and the UCP are still carried by police and SWAT, and the SV-98 by mercenary snipers. The UCP, a prototype that was never sold, no longer turns up in survivor and bandit bags.
Applies to containers and zombies that have not been looted or spawned yet.
