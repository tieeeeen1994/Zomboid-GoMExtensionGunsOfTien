3.17.0: SVU, SV-98 and SV-99

Main mod
- SVU (7.62x54mmR), the bullpup Dragunov of 1991 in its first, semi-automatic form, built on Guns of Marz's SVD:
  shortened barrel with its three-chamber muzzle brake, butt behind the magazine, grip and trigger under the
  handguard, cheek rest on the receiver cover. Folding iron sights, raised without an optic and folded under one.
  Takes Guns of Marz's SVD magazine, its AK mount (on the SVU's side rail, moved forward as on the real gun) and its
  optics. Reloads with Guns of Marz's bullpup (FAMAS) animation. Found where Guns of Marz's SVD is (Russian rifles,
  mercenary snipers).

Post-93 add-on
- SV-98 (7.62x54mmR), the Russian bolt-action sniper rifle of 2000 in its green plywood thumbhole stock: its own
  10-round magazine, a rail on the receiver for Guns of Marz optics, a bipod that folds back under the forend,
  worked by hand after every shot. Found with mercenary snipers and with Guns of Marz's post-1993 guns.
- SV-99 (.22 LR), the quiet sniper rifle with its straight-pull toggle lever and built-in suppressor, in green
  plywood with the open butt of its 2009 stock. 5-round magazine, a rail for Guns of Marz optics, a folding bipod.
  Found with Guns of Marz's post-1993 guns.
- The SV-98 and SV-99 reload with Guns of Marz's magazine bolt-action animation.
