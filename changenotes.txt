H&K HK416 (Post-93 add-on)
- New gun: the H&K HK416 D14.5RS, the piston-driven M4 of 2004 with a 14.5-inch barrel, as "HK416 Assault Rifle"
- Takes Guns of Marz's STANAG magazines (20 to 150 rounds) and its AR muzzle parts: the AR muzzle mount, the MKI and NDR suppressors, the compensator, flash hider and muzzle brake
- H&K's free-floating rail system with rails on all four sides: Guns of Marz optics on top, foregrips and bipods underneath, lights on the left, lasers on the right
- H&K's six-position stock: steadier and quicker to aim with it pulled out. H&K's grip, and its red and white fire mode labels on both sides of the ambidextrous selector
- Automatic or single shots at 850 rounds a minute. Hits like Guns of Marz's M4A1 but a little steadier, jams less and wears more slowly
- The flip-up iron sights fold away under an optic: the rear sight folds under a red dot or holographic sight (the front stays up and shows through it), both fold under a magnified scope
- Found where the SCAR-L is: in Guns of Marz's army rifle kits and on army zombies, with STANAG 30 magazines and boxes of 5.56x45mm

Iron sights under optics (Post-93 add-on)
- The MP7A1 and MP7A2, the SCAR-L and SCAR-H and the four Vectors now show their flip-up iron sights as the HK416 does: up with no optic, the rear folded under a red dot or holographic sight, both folded under a magnified scope
- Where an optic's mount sits on the rear sight's spot on the rail (the Elcan and the TR06X on the SCAR-L; the Kobra, the Elcan and the TR06X on the HK416), the rear sight is taken off instead
- The SCARs' rear sight now stands up when no optic is fitted, and the Vectors' sights stand up too (they were always folded)
- On the UMP45 and UMP9, the LR10X, LRX12X and PSO-1 sit further forward on the top rail, so their eyepieces clear the fixed rear sight
