Two new 9x39mm guns in the Post-93 add-on (3.23.0).

Post-93 add-on
- KBP 9A-91, the compact Russian police carbine: automatic or single shots at 700 rounds a minute, its own 20-round magazine, a stock that folds over the receiver, Guns of Marz's AK side mount on the left rail for any of its optics.
- KBP VSK-94, its sniper version on the same receiver: a skeleton stock, steadier and longer-ranged, the same magazine.
- Both take the 9A-91 suppressor on the new 9x39mm muzzle mount (as quiet as the AS VAL, since 9x39 is a subsonic round; it wears and is repaired like Guns of Marz's PBS-1). They fire Guns of Marz's 9x39mm rounds and the main mod's SP-6, reload with Guns of Marz's AK reload, and are found with Guns of Marz's Russian rifles (and with Undead Survivors' mercenaries); the suppressor and its mount turn up where Guns of Marz's suppressors do.

Also in this update (3.22.0):

Muzzle devices do their real job, and Guns of Marz's new tooltip wording (3.22.0).

Muzzle devices
- Every flash hider, compensator and muzzle brake now does more than lower the sound, Guns of Marz's LR2, LX and Trix42 included: compensators Aiming Time -3 and automatic fire spread -10%, muzzle brakes Aiming Time -5 and spread -5%, flash hiders Hit Chance +2%. Their tooltips list it.

Balance, as Guns of Marz
- Suppressor tooltips give Guns of Marz's new flat numbers for each calibre's usual gun (the PBS-1: Sound Radius reduced by 140 and Volume by 28), this mod's and the Post-93 add-on's suppressors too. The suppressors themselves are unchanged.
- The sawn-off stocks and the Mosin's sawn-off barrel give flat values. Sawn-off stock: Aiming Time -10, Hit Chance -8%, Recoil Delay +9, so the gun is again slower between shots and slower to steady, which had stopped working since Guns of Marz's November update. The Mosin's sawn-off stock alone now aims in a little slower than before.
