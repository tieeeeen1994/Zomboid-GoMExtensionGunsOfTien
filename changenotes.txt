3.14.0

.22 LR
- New calibre: the .22 Long Rifle, for the .22 guns to come (no gun fires it yet). In boxes of 50, cartons and crates, opened and packed like the other rounds
- The plain round is the common 40 grain lead round nose. Three special loads, each in its own boxes, cartons and crates:
- Subsonic: 15% less damage, 40% quieter, stacks with suppressors. Self-loading .22s have to be racked after every shot with it; revolvers are not affected
- Hollow Point: 20% more damage, harder critical hits, slightly more wear on the gun
- Hyper Velocity: 25% more damage, a little louder, more wear on the gun
- Like the other special loads they are not found as loot. With Ammo Maker every .22 LR load can be made, and with Hot Brass they throw .22 casings

Change
- New box art for the 4.6x30mm and 5.7x28mm rounds: each calibre has a layout of its own (the MP7A1 on 4.6x30mm DM11, the UCP Prototype on Subsonic, the "Iron Wasp" maker on 5.7x28mm)
- Five-SeveN and UCP Prototype (Post-93 add-on): Guns of Marz's three pistol lasers fit them now, as on the Glock 21. They go under the barrel where the pistol lights go, so a pistol takes a light or a laser
- The P90's 5.7x28mm SS190 round now lists in its tooltip that it pierces up to 2 targets
