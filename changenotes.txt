Two new calibres, the 7.62x25mm Tokarev and the 9x18mm Makarov, and in the Post-93 add-on KBP's A-91M bullpup with its grenade launcher (3.25.0).

Calibres
- 7.62x25mm Tokarev, the round of the Tokarev pistols and Soviet submachine guns: boxes of 50, cartons and crates, with a Steel Core load (pierces a second target) and a heavy Subsonic load. Its boxes carry the SAKER falcon.
- 9x18mm Makarov, the Soviet police and army pistol round: boxes of 50, cartons and crates, under the TUSKER boar.
- Both come ahead of their guns (the Tokarev pistol and PPSh-41 are next) and are made with Ammo Maker until then; they are not found as loot yet.

Post-93 add-on
- KBP A-91M, a 7.62x39mm bullpup with a 40mm grenade launcher under the barrel: Guns of Marz's AK magazines, automatic or single shots, switch to the launcher with Gunworks' underbarrel control (Guns of Marz's 40mm rounds), Guns of Marz's rail on the carrying handle for optics, AK muzzle devices.
- It is found with the Grozas: Russian rifle spawns, mercenaries and (with Extend Loot Areas) criminals.
