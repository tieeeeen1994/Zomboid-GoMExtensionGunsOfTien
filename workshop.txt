description=[h2]Changes to Guns of Marz[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Realistic sound radius and volume for the guns (not the grenade launchers)
description=[*]Plain .30-06, 7.62x51mm and .308 rounds pierce (sandbox option)
description=[*]7.62x39mm US (Subsonic) must be racked after each shot unless a PBS-1 is fitted (sandbox option)
description=[*]Lasers work: switch them on from the gun's menu or the firearm radial; while on, the laser shows a short red beam on the gun, also to other players; they run on a battery (about 3 weeks switched on, changed like a weapon light's) and give their aiming bonus only while on
description=[*]Clear tooltips: stock and bipod lines state what deploying them does; part rows that showed raw keys are named; every round's lines are written from its real stats; bayonets, the shell holder, the Picatinny Rail, speedloaders, slings and guns that had no lines say what they do
description=[*]AWC Mk9 suppressor for 9mm submachine guns, on its own muzzle mount: fits the MP5, MP5A2 and this mod's Uzis (Guns of Marz's 9mm suppressors fit only its pistols), found where Guns of Marz's suppressors and muzzle devices are
description=[*]Guns of Marz fixes: the Karabiner 98k bayonet fits the guns the other bayonets fit, and the overpressured 5.56's automatic fire spread penalty applies
description=[*]Sawn-off stocks: the break-action shotguns (Double Barrel, Stevens 555, TOZ-34) can have their wooden stock sawn off behind the pistol grip with any saw (Saw Off Shotgun Stock); lighter and quicker to aim in, but less accurate and slower between shots; with sawn-off barrels too they fit a holster. The sawn-off stock and barrel ends are kept as offcuts: wood to burn or make charcoal, steel to smelt. The Stevens 555's and TOZ-34's sawn-off barrels handle like the Double Barrel's
description=[*]9x39mm round renamed SP-5, 5.45x39mm round renamed 7N6
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]M249 Light Machine Gun and belt links[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]M249 Light Machine Gun (the 1993 PIP gun): full auto at 750 or 1000 rounds a minute (its gas regulator), fed from its own 200-round box or, in an emergency, any of Guns of Marz's STANAG magazines (which jam twice as often), integrated bipod; found where Guns of Marz's M60 is (army zombies, army storage, heavy army trucks), with its boxes
description=[*]Belt links: filling the M249's 200-round box or Guns of Marz's M60 100-round box now takes one belt link per round (M27 links for the M249, M13 for the M60). Unloading a round, firing it or racking it out gives its link back: fired and racked links are thrown out of the gun (with Hot Brass they fly and land like casings, without it they drop at your feet; Ammo Maker's brass catchers catch them). A belt box found loaded counts as linked
description=[*]Links come in boxes (200 M27, 100 M13) in the M249 and M60 kits and in army ammunition storage, or are made at any surface from a small sheet of metal with sheet metal snips, a hammer and a metalworking punch (Make M27 5.56mm or M13 7.62mm Belt Links, Metalworking 2: 50 M27 or 40 M13 links)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Uzi, Mini Uzi and Micro Uzi[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Uzi (held two-handed like the MP5, its metal stock folding under the receiver), Mini Uzi and Micro Uzi (held one-handed and fitting a holster like the MP5K and MAC-10, stocks folding along the right side; the Micro Uzi fits an ankle holster too): automatic at 600, 950 and 1200 rounds a minute, or single shots
description=[*]One set of IMI magazines fits all three: 20, 25 and 32 rounds, and long 40 and 50-round magazines
description=[*]The AWC Mk9 suppressor fits all three on its 9mm SMG muzzle mount
description=[*]Found with Guns of Marz's short SMGs (gun stores, pawn shops, survivalists, bandits); the Uzi also with its police SMGs and on SWAT zombies, and with its criminals' guns (criminal zombies, bandit bags, pawn shops, stashes); the long magazines with its high-capacity magazines
